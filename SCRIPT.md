# Blade on the Beat — Script

Short scenes between seven nights. Each scene is a few cards of text over an ink painting. Letters arrive tied to arrows, pinned to the gate, or left in the snow. Lines marked `[card]` are shown one at a time. The player taps to advance.

## Title

BLADE ON THE BEAT
Seven nights at Kestrel Gate

## Opening

[card] Above the village of Emberfall there is one road through the mountains, and one gate across it.

[card] Ren has kept Kestrel Gate for three winters. Nobody has come through in all that time.

[card] Tonight she hears drums in the north. She knows the rhythm. She learned it as a girl, in the camp of the Iron Choir.

[card] The Iron Choir fights to music. Every blow has its beat. A soldier who knows the song can meet each one.

[card] REN: Then I will learn their song again.

[card] Watch the wind-up. Strike when it lands.

## Night 1 — Scouts

Before: Two scouts test the gate.

After:

[card] The scouts run back into the dark. One drops a satchel. Inside is a crust of bread, black with mould, carried like treasure.

[card] REN: Nobody carries bread like that unless they are starving.

LETTER, found in the satchel:

> Ren,
> They tell me a swordswoman holds Kestrel Gate. They tell me she parries on the beat and never on the count before. I taught only one student to wait like that.
> Stand aside. I do not want to meet you on the road.
> — A.

## Night 2 — The Drummer

Before:

[card] Hadd the Drummer carries a war drum as tall as a door. His army marches to it, and so will his blows.

[card] He hits hard, and he hits between the beats.

After:

[card] The great drum splits. Hadd sits down in the snow and laughs until he coughs.

[card] HADD: Aldric said you would do that. He was proud of it, girl. He still is.

[card] REN: Then why is he coming?

[card] HADD: Ask him what we ate this winter.

LETTER, pinned to the gate with a drumstick:

> Ren,
> The rivers froze in the ninth month. The herds died in the tenth. We have buried more children than soldiers.
> Emberfall has a granary. I have an army that is hungry. I wish those two facts did not meet at your gate.
> — Aldric

## Night 3 — The Twin Archers

Before:

[card] Sif and Sela never miss, and they never shoot together. One calls, the other answers.

[card] Listen for the side. The left bow sings low. The right bow sings high.

After:

[card] The twins lower their bows at the same moment. It is the first thing they have done together all night.

[card] SELA: Our mother is in the camp. She has not stood up in a week.

[card] Ren gives them her own rations and sends them north before dawn.

Word from the village:

> Keeper,
> We saw the arrows from the square. The children are asking if the Choir is coming down. What should I tell them?
> — Maren, headwoman of Emberfall

## Night 4 — The Night Raid

Before:

[card] No champion tonight. Torch-bearers run for the wall to set Emberfall alight while Ren is busy at the gate.

[card] The torches aim past her, at the village. Stop every one you can.

After:

[card] Smoke hangs over the rooftops until morning. Maren climbs the road with a bucket still in her hand.

[card] MAREN: We put the fires out. Some of them.

[card] REN: They are not cruel. They are hungry.

[card] MAREN: Hungry people burned my neighbour's roof, Ren.

[card] Neither of them is wrong.

## Night 5 — The Masked Dancer

Before:

[card] The Dancer moves like a song played slightly late. The Choir says no one has ever found the beat under the mask.

[card] Feel the swing. Wait for the long note.

After:

[card] The mask falls. Underneath is Iyo, who shared Ren's tent when they were both children in the camp.

[card] IYO: You left. We stayed. That is the whole difference between us.

[card] REN: Stay here, then. With me.

[card] Iyo looks at the lights of Emberfall for a long moment, then walks north.

LETTER, left in the snow:

> Ren,
> You always asked why I made you wait for the beat instead of swinging first. It is because the one who waits sees everything. I am asking you to see my people now.
> Two more nights. Then I come myself.
> — Aldric

## Night 6 — The Ram

Before:

[card] The Choir brings a ram of black pine. Its crew swings in threes while the ram strikes in twos.

[card] Every blow on the gate shakes the village below it.

After:

[card] The ram cracks and rolls back down the road. Its crew does not chase it.

[card] Ren writes a letter of her own, and leaves it where the scouts will find it.

> Teacher,
> Come to the gate tomorrow, alone if you can. Whatever happens, I will not swing first.
> — Ren

## Night 7 — Aldric

Before:

[card] He comes alone, as she asked. He is thinner than she remembers.

[card] ALDRIC: You kept the gate well.

[card] REN: You taught me.

[card] ALDRIC: Then you know I will not make this easy.

[card] He uses every rhythm his army played. Some of his wind-ups are lies. A pale blade is a feint. Do not answer it.

## Ending — The Open Granary

Shown when enough of Emberfall is still standing.

[card] Aldric's sword stops a finger's width from her shoulder. Hers stops at his.

[card] Behind them, Emberfall is still lit. Maren is walking up the road with a lantern, and behind her come carts.

[card] MAREN: We have more grain than a village this size can eat. Nobody has ever asked us for it.

[card] ALDRIC: I did not ask. I came with a sword.

[card] REN: Then ask now.

[card] He lowers his blade.

[card] That winter the gate stays open. The Iron Choir comes down the mountain singing, and nobody in Emberfall needs to fear the drums again.

## Ending — The Long Road North

Shown when too much of Emberfall has burned.

[card] Aldric falls to one knee. Ren does not strike.

[card] Below them, half of Emberfall is ash. The granary roof has fallen in.

[card] ALDRIC: There is nothing left to take. Or to give.

[card] REN: I held the gate. I could not hold the village.

[card] He stands, bows to his student, and walks north with the little the villagers can spare.

[card] In spring, Ren rebuilds the wall and sends seed north. She does not know if anyone is there to plant it.

## Defeat

[card] Ren falls. The drums keep playing.
